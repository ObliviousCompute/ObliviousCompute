# Appendix B — Axioms

## Purpose

This appendix establishes the fundamental axioms from which the theory is derived. Each axiom is presented in both natural language and mathematical form.

## Axiom 1 — Genesis

Every admissible progression begins from a genesis.

### Mathematical Form

Given a cell c,

g ∈ F

Every admissible progression originates from g.

---

## Axiom 2 — Transition

Progression occurs exclusively through transitions.

### Mathematical Form

T : sᵢ → sᵢ₊₁

Every admissible progression is composed of transitions.

---

## Axiom 3 — Admissibility

Only admissible transitions contribute to reality.

### Mathematical Form

A(T(s)) = true

Only admissible transitions may produce a new admissible state.

---

## Axiom 4 — Invariance

Every admissible transition preserves the system invariants.

### Mathematical Form

A(T(s)) ⇒ I(s) = I(T(s))

---

## Axiom 5 — Verification

Admissibility is established through verification.

### Mathematical Form

V(T(s)) = A(T(s))

---

## Axiom 6 — Event Horizon

Every cell possesses exactly one current event horizon.

### Mathematical Form

∀c, ∃! E(c)

---

## Axiom 7 — Reality

Reality consists exclusively of presently admissible states.

### Mathematical Form

R = { s ∈ F | A(s) }

---

## Axiom 8 — Synchronization

Observers synchronize by verifying admissible progression.

### Mathematical Form

Synchronization preserves admissible reality without requiring historical reconstruction.
